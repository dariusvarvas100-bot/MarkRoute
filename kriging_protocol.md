# Terrain-aware local kriging comparator

Freeze this extension before running the comparator on any held-out event. The existing MarkRoute benchmark and its held-out results were already known. The comparator’s predictions and scores have not been inspected.

Reuse the public processed panels and existing 15% split, projected geography, environment separation, seed range, and up to 12 physically nearest surveyed anchors per withheld target. The development comparison uses Florence and Michael with 10 masks. The original (Ian, Helene) and additional (Ida, Texas, Pacific Northwest) tests each use the published 16 masks per event. Neither the kriging nor the original models may access withheld elevations. Same-storm anchor elevations are known to all methods at prediction time.

Model the measured elevation minus a terrain trend using local ordinary kriging. The terrain slope uses either the physical starting value (0 at the coast, 1 on rivers) or the existing locally bounded slope fit to the available anchors. On the residual, use a Matérn-3/2 covariance over projected positions with an optional vertical distance term and a positive same-waterbody covariance boost. The GP neighborhood is intentionally limited to the same anchor information used by the published model. A constant residual mean is estimated by generalized least squares per target. The constant ensures predictions reproduce a constant residual at sparse or distant points.

Try exactly 16 combinations, determined by slope variant (physics or bounded), a horizontal scale of 0.75 or 2.5 times the median target-to-anchor distance with a 0.25 km minimum, a vertical term off or 45 ft, and a nugget of 0.03 or 0.25 relative to unit signal variance. The same named-waterbody kernel gives weight 1 to known shared labels and 0.35 otherwise, with covariance diagonal 1 before the nugget. Select by pooled validation MAE, tie-break by fixed enumeration order. No tuning on held-out storms. The released model remains the frozen gradient-boosted residual estimator for this comparison.

Report the MAE and >5-ft miss rate against frozen MarkRoute and the original bounded regression, pooled and by event, for both archived cohorts separately. Also report combined paired-mask MAE and equal-event-weighted MAE, plus 95% intervals from the published 10 km paired block bootstrap. These spatial intervals describe archived-storm comparisons. Repeat a same-mask sentinel test to verify hidden elevations do not change predictions. Verify the original MAEs and cohort sizes against the published snapshot before interpreting comparator results.
