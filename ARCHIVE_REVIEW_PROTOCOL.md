# Withholding provisional estimates

This follow-up asks how many large misses remain when the frozen estimator declines to report the least defensible estimates. It reuses the nine-event archive and its 16 initial-survey masks. No method is treated as a guarantee. The point-model results for these floods were already known before this follow-up.

Within each event, environment, and mask, rank estimates using only geometry, terrain, and predictions from the shared starting measurements. Consider low model disagreement with bounded regression, low local relief, short distance to the nearest surveyed mark, and an equally weighted mean of the three within-group ranks. Choose a ranking on riverine marks in Florence and Michael by the lowest observed rate of errors greater than five feet when reporting the lowest-risk 20%. Freeze the ranking before the additional archives are scored. Also report the development choice that ranks first at each other budget.

Report 5%, 10%, 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, and 100% nominal reporting coverage on the nine archival floods. Keep at least one estimate per mask and environment. Give the actual coverage, count of errors larger than five feet and mean absolute error overall and for each event. This is retrospective selective prediction. It does not estimate field crew time or provide new-site coverage guarantees.
