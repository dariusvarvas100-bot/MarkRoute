# MarkRoute additional-event evaluation

The original training and validation storms remain the only model development data. Hold the corrected mapping pipeline, its fixed gradient boosting parameters, the 15% initial budget, the 16 existing seed choices, and bounded local regression fixed. Evaluate a separate catalog-selected cohort without altering the original holdout. Each event retains the original quality, NAVD88, distinct site, and distinct small-grid-cell criteria.

Selection uses the archived catalog as of November 14, 2026. Consider every event with nonmissing start and end dates and at least 120 days after its end. Select USGS catalog type 2 storms starting in 2020 or later, or catalog type 1 floods starting after Helene's September 24, 2024 start date. Exclude the eight original development and test events. Events with fewer than 30 retained marks, unavailable source data, or unresolved terrain acquisition are ineligible. Keep a manifest of all candidates and exact exclusions. No event eligibility criterion uses the measured elevation value except whether it exists.

Compare mean absolute surface elevation error in feet, the >5-ft error rate, severe tail quantiles, within-event and pooled location-mask pairs, and a macro average over events. Compute original within-event spatial block intervals and an exploratory paired interval resampling entire events. No new parameters are tuned. Passing the benchmark requires at least 10% lower pooled MAE, a positive paired spatial interval, and no event more than 5% worse. Event-level intervals describe only this cohort. Reconstruction assumes the sites and environmental flag metadata are already known and says nothing about unsurveyed sites, future storm prediction, navigability, or water depths at the site.
